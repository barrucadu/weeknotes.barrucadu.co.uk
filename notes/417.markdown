## Miscellaneous

I have fallen down the mechanical keyboard switch rabbit hole today.

For some reason Youtube recommended me a bunch of videos about people's desk
set-ups, I put one on while eating lunch the other day, and it was like Eve
eating the apple: I was cursed with knowledge.

You see, I've had a kind of unconscious dissatisfaction with my keyboard for a
while.  It's a full-size mechanical keyboard that I've had for probably over a
decade at this point.  It does feel nice to type on, but it's quite loud.  I
intentionally type more softly when on voice calls as it is picked up by my
microphone.  And it's large, but I never use the numpad so that's just wasted
space.

So I've found myself looking at smaller keyboards.  But I'd also want it to be
quiet enough that a microphone won't really pick it up, but nothing comes with
silent switches by default, so I want a keyboard that has swappable switches.
And if I'm swapping the out switches I need to know which ones to get.  And
there's way more types of switch than there were when I got my current keyboard
back in the day...

I think I have an idea for what I want to get, but I'm going to give it a little
while as while a smaller, quieter, keyboard would be *nice* it's not exactly
*necessary*, and it will end up being a few hundred pounds.
