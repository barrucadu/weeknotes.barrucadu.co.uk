## Books

This week I read:

- [The King of Elfland's Daughter][]

  I think this is probably the best "real-world fairytale" ever written.  Lord
  Dunsany is great at this sort of writing (as aptly demonstrated in his more
  fantastical stories, which themselves inspired Lovecraft's dreamlands), and
  *The King of Elfland's Daughter* takes that style of writing but grounds it
  *just enough* to fit in some nonspecific part of fairytale-England.  Another
  great book along the same lines is his *The Blessing of Pan* which I'm now
  feeling a craving to re-read.

## Miscellaneous

I set up a little home gym in my conservatory back in July.  I wasn't using the
space for anything, and I've periodically tried to get fitter but always fallen
out of the habit, so I thought why not give it another try but add on a bit of
sunk cost fallacy for motivation?

Well, seven weeks in it appears to be working.  I've been following the [Hybrid
Calisthenics][] routine, along with alternating [Couch to 5k][] and a basic
dumbbell routine (bicep curls, cross-body hammer curls, lateral raises, and
rows).  I have one rest day a week which moves around based on what I'm doing,
but is usually Tuesday.  Things that were very hard at the beginning are now
much less hard, I've been able to follow Couch to 5k as written, and I've
progressed to harder variations of the calisthenics exercises and to heavier
weights for the strength workout.

A few weeks in I started stretching in the morning with the goal of being able
to touch my toes, but I haven't made any progress with that at all and the
stretches don't feel challenging, so I think I need to find a different routine.
But the workout routine is working well.

[Hybrid Calisthenics]: https://www.hybridcalisthenics.com/routine
[Couch to 5k]: https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/get-active/get-running-with-couch-to-5k/couch-to-5k-running-plan/
