## Roleplaying Games

### How Springs Island

This week the players got the basic story from the nereids Chlora and Idriel
that they'd rescued, but as the nereids had been trapped for thousands of years
they were totally unaware that the free nereids now were allied with the Night
Axe ogres and enemies of Svarku, who has 17 captive nereids in his harem.  So
when the players encountered a bunch of ogres later in the session, they kept
their distance.  More than that, they've decided to go to the volcano to attend
this New Moon Party that Trouble, the salamander, had invited them too.

We ended the session with them being accosted on the slopes of the volcano by a
bunch of salamanders and fire imps and being made to admire the great statues of
Svarku in his four aspects of warrior, saviour, poet, and wizard.

It's the night of the new moon.  I can't wait for the players to meet Svarku and realise:

- He's obscenely wealthy and free with his wealth to those who please with him.
- He's both individually powerful *and* has an army of several hundred fire elementals.
- He has 17 of the nereids captive, which immediately pits the players against him.

Fun times ahead!

I don't know how long this campaign will last, it feels like it's going fairly
quickly with the players about to hit upon the core conflict of the setting in
only 8 sessions, so I suppose I'd better start thinking about what comes next.
