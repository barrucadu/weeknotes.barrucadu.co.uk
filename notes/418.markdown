## Roleplaying Games

### Asher's Ridge

This week I and one of the other players tried out [Asher's Ridge][], which is a
GMless 1--4 player game designed to produce an episode of a small-town paranomal
mystery TV show, like Twin Peaks (or other shows it references that I've not
watched).  Specifically, it produces *episodes of a show*, you're not just
roleplaying the characters but also making explicit reference to TV show
concepts such as "loglines", "scenes", and "acts" to structure the session.
It's got some interesting mechanics too, being completely diceless, instead
using a deck of cards and a set of Scrabble tiles.

We found it a little slow to get into, mostly due to lack of familiarity with
the source material, but I think once we got over that hump and things started
to flow it worked pretty well.  I definitely want to come back to it in the
future, not necessarily for a campaign but definitely for more one-shots.

[Asher's Ridge]: https://www.montfordtales.com/ashers-ridge/

![Hedgehog in the hoghouse!](notes/418/hoghouse.png)

In other news, I've been trying to cut down on how much frozen food I eat.  I
used to always have chips, pizzas, pies, (etc) in the freezer ostensibly for
emergencies but in practice I ate that sort of thing quite often.

Last month I started freezing sliced bread (I already bake my own bread) so if I
was out of all other food I could easily just make a sandwich, and it has worked
wonderfully.  I've not bought anything frozen since.  All I need to do is bake
bread a little more often than before.
