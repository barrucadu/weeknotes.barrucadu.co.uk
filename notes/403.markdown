## Roleplaying Games

### Hot Springs Island

I was very pleased to see the trend of the players looking creatures up in the
Field Guide after I described them continued this week.  We got some good
adventuring done, and ended the session teaming up with a pair of NPCs (one of
whom doesn't speak the language) to go raid a dungeon.  I'll have to prepare
some character sheets for them and figure out why they were interested (it was a
random encounter) by next time---one of the NPCs is a water wizard, and there is
a nereid trapped in the dungeon, so perhaps I'll go for an "I sensed a
disturbamce in the force" type thing, which can also be a way for the players to
learn that the magic painting *they've* got is also a nereid.

I think it's about time for me to leave a magic weapon lying around for them to
find too, they've levelled up a couple of times now and had their first run-in
with a creature immune to nonmagical damnage.
