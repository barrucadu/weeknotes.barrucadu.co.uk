## Books

This week I read:

- Volume 6 of the [Rozen Maiden][] anniversary edition by Peach-Pit

  It's hard to know what to write about Rozen Maiden, 80+ chapters in, because
  the story is just very high-context.  Describing the events of one book
  wouldn't make any sense at all.  That said, it continues to be good, and much
  better than the anime adaptation which I feel abbreviated the end of the story
  far too much.

[Rozen Maiden]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rozen_Maiden


## Roleplaying Games

We're back to alternating campaigns: this week one of the players started
running D&D 4e, next week I'll be starting a Secrets of the Ancients campaign
using Traveller.  I've been reading through the book and making preparations and
I think it'll be a good time.  On the whole it's a fun adventure, but there are
a few moments where the players *need* to make a certain decision for the game
to work... Thankfully, Seth Skorkowsky has an amazing [campaign diary series][]
covering it which addresses those points, so I'm at least going in prepared.

[campaign diary series]: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL25p5gPY6qKW-7aW5rCx62j7LH_SDgA4_
