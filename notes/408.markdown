## Books

This week I read:

- [The Fellowship of the Ring][] by J. R. R. Tolkien

  Hot on the heels of The Hobbit, I decided to re-read the rest of LotR, and wow
  there's a lot which is *slightly different* to the film that I'd forgotten.
  The film is a very good adaptation, in my opinion, and does hit a lot of the
  major points of the book.  And there are major differences everyone knows
  about, like shortening the time between the Birthday Party and Frodo leaving
  the Shire, or totally omitting Tom Bombadill and the Barrow-Downs, but there
  are also many other differences that I'd forgotten.

  In some cases the order of events is slightly different, compressed or
  rearranged in the film for timing reasons, but sometimes it's pretty big.
  Moria in particular is quite different: *Gandalf* was the one who proposed
  going through Moria (Caradhras was Aragorn's idea), and Pippin throws a rock
  down the well quite early in their journey with the goblins only showing up
  days later.  Aragorn gets Anduril when they set off from Rivendell, and
  there's a lot less tension between him and Boromir.  Gimli is much less comic
  relief, Legolas jokes more.  Some major scenes are covered incredibly briefly
  too, the entire fight on Weathertop is only a couple of paragraphs; and yet we
  get pages upon pages of walking.

[The Fellowship of the Ring]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring
