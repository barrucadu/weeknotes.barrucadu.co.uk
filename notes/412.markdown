## Books

This week I read:

- [The Return of the King][] by J. R. R. Tolkien

  The thrilling final instalment!  I always forget just how long the time spent
  hanging around in Gondor is after the defeat of Sauron, I *know* it's quite a
  while, but it's always more than that.  But I think it's necessary for the
  book, everyone deserves an epilogue.  I have mixed feelings about the film
  omitting the Scouring of the Shire.  It's a very important part of the story
  thematically, I feel; that war isn't only something that happens in far off
  lands, in foreign parts, places where people are *different*, that it can
  strike even the cosy familiarity of the British countryside.  Sorry, I mean
  Hobbiton countryside, Tolkien was *quite clear* that there is *absolutely
  nothing allegorical* in The Lord of the Rings.  I can see why it was left out
  of the film, it was that or cut something else, or make a fourth film, but is
  a shame.

  The appendices are something else.  I'm not sure I've ever read them fully
  before.  I wasn't expecting an explanation of how the names were translated.

## Roleplaying Games

### Hot Springs Island

W're still playing alternate weeks, so no game this week.  But I did spend a few
hours preparing the wider Swordfish Islands setting.

I rolled up a village for [Scenic Dunnsmouth][] and reskinned it as a research
expedition: there's two research groups who hate each other (and each considers
*their* group in charge around here), a group of poorly-paid labourers, and the
wealthy expedition funders.  They're here to study tropical swamp animals.

I also used [Into the Wyrd and Wild][] to roll up a little pointcrawl for
another of the islands, but I'm not completely sold on it.  It needs some work.

Anyway, I've determined where all the nereids are trapped and have at least
vague ideas of adventures for each of them, so I can just flesh these out a few
at a time and deliver adventure hooks to the players as I have them.

## Miscellaneous

Good news everyone, the hedgehog is back!

Or at least, something is eating the food.  I think it's most likely a hedgehog:
I can't think of many small (no larger than hedgehog-sized) nocturnal creatures
that frequent British suburbs and willingly crawl into small ground-level holes.
Birds don't like enclosed spaces.  Bats prefer to be high up.  Squirrels aren't
nocturnal, and the squirrel that visits the garden prefers stealing nuts from
the birds anyway.  So even though I haven't seen it, I think on the balance of
probabilities it is likely a hedgehog.

![I put a smaller pile of food by the entrance to entice any passing 'hogs.](notes/412/hedgehog-food.jpg)

In trying-out-new-software news, I'm still happy with [donetick][] and
[vikunja][] as a Trello replacement, and I've made some more changes this week.

Firstly, I set up [Authelia][] for SSO, now that I have donetick and vikunja and
grafana which all have their own user management.  I'm using the [file backend
for Authelia][authelia-file] and have all the users (which is just me) defined
in my NixOS secrets, giving me declarative user management.

Secondly, I'm trying out [hister][] as a replacement for my basic home-made
[bookmarks search engine][bookmarks].  I've not really had chance to play around
with it yet, but it certainly *looks* nice.  I've got it up and running with
backups and SSO, and have gone through all my bookmarks and imported the small
fraction of them that I was actually interested in keeping.  I have a couple of
blogs I want to look through too to make sure I have everything interesting
stored ([ACOUP][] and [The Great Dungeon of the North][]).

Something I found a bit sad while looking through my existing 1000+ bookmarks is
that a *lot* of the links are now dead.  One thing that's cool about Hister is
that it stores a local copy of the page HTML and shows that in the search
results, so even if a page is gone, I can still at least get the text content.

I'm toying with the idea of setting up a feed reader too, so I can migrate off
[The Old Reader][], but I haven't looked into options at all yet.
