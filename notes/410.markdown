## Books

This week I read:

- [HELLPIERCERS][]

  A roleplaying game set after humanity has cast down the demiurge and reclaimed
  its inherent divinity, only to turn around and make war on Hell for the
  liberation of the dead.  This game oozes flavour.  It's a tactical skirmish
  game which divides the game up into three modes of play: RECON, which is what
  I would consider normal RPG play; TACCOM, which is the tactical combat mode;
  and STRATCOM, which is kind of a planning and base-building mode in which both
  the players and the forces of Hell will modify the map and unlock units and
  powers for upcoming RECON and TACCOM phases.

  Players are powerful.  Most demonic minions go down easily, but there are a
  lot of them.  Minibosses, boss monsters, video-gane-like puzzles, and
  interesting map design (the GM section on TACCOM explicitly calls out raids in
  MMOs as inspiration) is how you keep it from being just a boring slog of
  mowing down grunts.

  It's very different to anything I've played or run before, and I am looking
  forward to getting it to the table.

[HELLPIERCERS]: https://hellpiercers.com/

hedgehog likes the paths (makes sense, when you're so small, the plants are very

![Hedgehog house: a hedgehouse, if you will.](notes/410/hedgehouse.jpg)
