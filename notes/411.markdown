## Roleplaying Games

### Hot Springs Island

This week I gave my players the run-down on the rest of the Swordfish Islands,
as I had finally read [Shipwrecked][].  They can go explore Marlo's Mire and
chat with the wizard if they want---I said there's a weekly ship to / from
Swordfish Port---though I have decided my version of Marlo's Mire doesn't have
the airship, but that should be a fairly minor adjustment; and I've said that
there will be adventures on the other islands, but likely not full hexcrawls.

I'm going to set [Scenic Dunnsmouth][] on the creepy spiderweb island (I'll
replace the spider-people with the spider cultists from the adventure), and I'm
going to scatter 10 of the nereid paintings around the rest.

Anyway, this week was spent mostly exploring the Lapis Observatory.  The players
found a magic sword, killed a giant snake, were imprinted upon by a tall naked
golem, befriended a lizardman, and made off with thousands of gold worth of
gaudy decorations.  A pretty successful time really.
